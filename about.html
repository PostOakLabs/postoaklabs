About

Research and publications

What the team has written and presented

Books

Several books on blockchain and distributed ledgers, including an early guide to smart contracts and trustless asset management (2014) and an analysis of bitcoin as a money-like informational commodity. Both remain standard references.

Permissioned blockchain

A 2015 paper, "Consensus-as-a-service", is credited with popularizing the term "permissioned blockchain" and the distinction between public and permissioned ledgers that institutional design still uses.

CBDC and central banks

Senior involvement in CBDC initiatives: leading a global CBDC advisory function at a major technology firm, contributing to a widely referenced central bank blockchain pilot, and advising on wholesale CBDC design across jurisdictions.

Cross-border payments

Hands-on development of a bank-led blockchain payment network that became one of the most cited examples of production tokenized remittance infrastructure.

Stablecoin policy

Analysis of reserve structures, systemic risk and regulatory design, cited in policy discussions and by regulators, including algorithmic stability mechanisms and collateral trade-offs.

Blockchain analytics

Research on transaction graph analysis, entity clustering and the limits of blockchain privacy, which informed analytics tools used by bank compliance teams.

Consensus design

Technical writing on finality versus throughput and how consensus choice affects institutional use cases, across proof-of-work, proof-of-stake and BFT designs.

Tokenized finance

Writing on tokenized money market funds, bonds and securities: settlement, regulatory treatment and adoption constraints.

Regulatory technology

Work from the supervisory side as well as the implementation side, including academic instruction in RegTech and the use of onchain datasets for compliance.
